01 / WHAT I DID

Shaping Social Media Presence Around Growth & Marketing Thought

I worked on shaping GrooHub’s social media presence to better reflect the way we think about marketing and growth. I built the strategy, planned the content, and directed the creative execution across designs and videos.

I also supervised studio shoots, making sure the content aligned with the creative direction, and came up with creative and different ways to capture and showcase client reviews.
