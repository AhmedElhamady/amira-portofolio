01 / THE CHALLENGE

Bridging Established Medical Expertise with Modern Digital Presence

Dr. Gasser already had a strong reputation in his field, but that wasn’t fully reflected in his social media presence. In the healthcare sector, trust, professional clarity, and patient-centered communication are vital.

I started by deeply understanding his goals, clinical focus, and patient audience, then established a strategic roadmap designed to turn complex medical information into accessible, reassuring, and engaging content.

02 / WHAT I DID

Strategic Planning & Creative Medical Storytelling

Audience & Goal Analysis

Analyzed target patients and audience inquiries to build tailored messaging that addresses real concerns and patient questions.

Content Plan & Strategy

Structured high-authority educational pillars, surgical awareness topics, and consistent posting calendars.

Video & Shoot Direction

Guided on-camera presence, talking points, lighting, and pacing for medical reels that inspire patient confidence.

Visual Design & Branding

Designed clean, medical-grade visual layouts and infographics with clear typography and professional color harmony.
